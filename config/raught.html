Pairing reboots the puck into Bluetooth pairing mode; then hold Change Grip/Order on the console. Each of these restarts the puck, so this page disconnects afterwards.

Waking the console

Hold the QAM button — the three dots between the trackpads — to wake a sleeping Switch. The puck borrows the radio, wakes it, clears the lock screen, and hands the radio straight back.

Chimes

What the controller plays before a wake, and before the puck reboots into another USB mode from the back 4 + L1 + R1 chord. Each note plays on the right rumble motor unless you pick another haptic. Unlike the other settings, notes only save when you press Save. Preview plays what is below without saving it, and needs the controller connected.

Wake timing

Counted in reports, roughly 30 ms each. Pressing too early achieves nothing — the console has woken but the lock screen is not up yet.

Extra buttons

The Pro Controller has no back paddles and no QAM button, so each one can stand in for a button it does have. They ship unmapped, because a button you rest a finger on is worse than one that does nothing.

Colours

How the pad looks on the console's controller screen. The console reads these once, during its handshake — unplug and replug the puck to see a change.

Trackpad mouse

The right trackpad moves a cursor. It tracks your thumb one to one; gliding only happens after you flick and lift.
